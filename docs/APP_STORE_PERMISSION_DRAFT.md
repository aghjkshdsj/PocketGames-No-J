# Proposed permission for Dan-controlled code

Review draft only. This file is not an adopted additional permission, EULA,
copyright assignment or change to LICENSE / LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md.

## Proposed wording for review

To the extent Dan owns the copyright in a contribution to PocketGames No J,
Dan proposes granting the following additional permission under GPL version 3
section 7 for that contribution:

> You may convey that contribution as part of PocketGames No J through Apple's
> App Store, including distribution by Apple and its distribution affiliates,
> under the store's required distribution conditions, usage rules and technical
> delivery restrictions. Those store-imposed conditions alone do not violate
> the GPL as applied to that contribution under this additional permission.
> This permission does not reduce obligations concerning source delivery,
> attribution, notices or materials needed to modify and relink LGPL libraries.
> It does not grant rights in contributions owned by anyone else, Apple's
> software, games, or Valve's software and services. Other distribution of that
> contribution remains available under its existing GPL grant and permissions.

The final wording must identify the actual rights holder and approved scope.
Existing GPL rights in previously distributed copies remain available.

## Scope to verify before adopting anything

Dan confirmed that he created both earlier projects and that the older names
credited to the project are his aliases. This supports investigating a permission
covering his app, port, Dock, interpreter integration and fork modifications.
It does not establish ownership of every line in those repositories.

Check copied code, imported commits, contributor agreements, dependency source
headers, and AI-assisted material whose underlying source is uncertain. Keep
Wine, GMP, Nettle, GnuTLS, FFmpeg, FreeType, LLVM, Blink and other upstream grants
separate. In particular, no proposed permission from Dan waives their LGPL
replacement, reverse-engineering-for-debugging or source obligations.

Do not insert this draft into the app's active license bundle or App Store
Connect. Review the ownership inventory, complete distribution arrangement and
actual account agreements first. A custom EULA also needs Apple's minimum
terms and the developer's actual legal identity and support contact details.

Sources: [GPL section 7](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/gpl-3.0/),
[Apple developer agreement, Schedule 1 and Exhibit B](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/).
